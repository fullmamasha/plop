# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. This is a small project about uploading files; there
is nothing here worth being unpleasant over.

## What is expected

- Assume the other person is acting in good faith.
- Criticise the code, the design or the idea, never the person.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no, and that "no" is a complete answer.
- Keep it on topic. Issues are about Plop.

## What is not accepted

Harassment of any kind, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised
language or imagery, publishing anyone's private information, and sustained
disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commits and anywhere else the project
is being discussed or represented.

## Reporting

If someone is behaving this way, message
[@fullmamasha](https://github.com/fullmamasha) on GitHub. Reports are handled
privately. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block
accounts, and is not obliged to explain each decision in public.
