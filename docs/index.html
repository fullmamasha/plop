Plop

A clipboard-first file upload extension for Chrome. Click an upload button and Plop opens instead of the system dialog — offering what you just copied, the files you pinned, and what you uploaded recently.

Add to Chrome GitHub Privacy policy
  • Clipboard first Whatever you copied is the first thing you see. Images arrive with a preview, text with an excerpt.
  • Paste anything Files copied in Explorer or Finder never reach the web clipboard API. Press Ctrl+V with Plop open and they come through.
  • Pinned files and recents Keep the files you send often, and reuse the last dozen you uploaded, without opening a dialog.
  • Anywhere, with a shortcut Press Ctrl+Shift+Space (⌘+Shift+Space on a Mac) and Plop opens at the pointer. Text goes into the field you are typing in; files go wherever the page takes them.
  • Per-site control Turn Plop off on a site that does not suit it, or everywhere, from the extensions menu.
  • Nothing leaves your device Plop has no server and no analytics. Your clipboard and your files stay on your computer.
